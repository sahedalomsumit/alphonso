The 72-hour rule

From branch to breakfast

A mango keeps ripening after it's picked. Most supply chains stop that clock with cold. We let it run — carefully, and on a timer.

  1. 01

    Netted at dawn

    Pickers climb before the heat, twisting each fruit free rather than pulling. Anything under 12° brix stays on the branch.

    Hour 0
  2. 02

    Rested, not chilled

    Fruit is laid in rice straw and held at 12°C — warm enough that flavour keeps developing, cool enough that nothing spoils.

    Hour 26
  3. 03

    Ripe when it arrives

    Boxed at the orchard gate, flown at night, and on your counter still firm to the squeeze. Two days on the shelf and it's perfect.

    Hour 72
